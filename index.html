[02 / The venture]Founder & CEO · July 2024 — Present

Circles IN

“Find your circle. Live your moment.”Getting the community back together!

Meeting people shouldn’t depend on luck

Finding things to do nearby — and people who enjoy the same things — is harder than it should be. Circles IN is a social events platform built to change that: discover and join local events that match your interests, connect with like-minded people, or host your own gatherings.

The mission is simple: genuine connections, built through shared experiences.

  • 01Discover & join local social events
  • 02Interest matching — activities that fit you
  • 03Host & manage your own events
  • 04Event chats and communities
  • 05Attendee lists, instant photo sharing & live updates

  1. Phase 01

    Before

    Discover events, see who’s going, and join the conversation in the event chat.

  2. Phase 02

    During

    Live updates and instant photo sharing keep everyone in the same moment.

  3. Phase 03

    After

    The circle stays together — communities that outlast a single event.

The founder’s role

  • Building the social networking and event-discovery platform end to end.
  • Led development and launch of the MVP in approximately two months.
  • Shaping event discovery, chats, attendee lists, photo sharing and live updates.
  • Supporting community growth across university and city communities.
  • Organised Circles Skatefest at Cornell University Reported 100% turnout
